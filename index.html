Who We Are

Not your average
book club.

We're two friends who kept texting each other book recommendations at 1am and decided to make it a thing. The Book Un Club is part reading group, part literary newsletter, part excuse to sit somewhere nice and talk for three hours.

We pick one book a month, share a playlist to read it by, write about it on Substack, and then meet up to dissect it properly. Everyone is welcome — whether you finished the book or just read the first chapter and have hot takes anyway.

📅 Monthly Meets

We gather once a month to discuss the pick. Find our next meetup on Meetup.com — open to all.

🎵 Reading Playlists
